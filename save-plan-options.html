SAVE is ending. Here are your five options, in real dollars.

Under Public Law 119-21, SAVE borrowers are being moved off the plan in rolling waves, each with a personal decision window of roughly 90 days. Do nothing and you're assigned a plan by default. Choose, and the differences compound for decades: payment formula, interest treatment, forgiveness clock, and taxes. Here's each option, computed from the text of the law.

The one-way door: under current rules, leaving SAVE or IBR for RAP is permanent — you cannot return. Run your numbers before you request anything, and confirm the payment on your servicer's site. Their numbers control your account.

RAP — Repayment Assistance Plan

  • Your yearly payment is 1–10% of AGI, set by income bracket; divided by 12, then reduced $50/month for each tax dependent. Floor: $10/month — RAP is never $0.
  • Unpaid interest is waived — your balance cannot grow. If your payment cuts principal by less than $50 in a month, the government tops up the difference.
  • Forgiveness after 360 qualifying payments (30 years). PSLF-eligible.
  • Excluded: Parent PLUS loans and consolidations that repaid one.

IBR — the other income-driven survivor

  • Protects 150% of the federal poverty line for your family size ($15,960 + $5,680 per additional person in 2026, contiguous US), then takes 10% of what's above it (first federal loan after July 1, 2014) or 15% (earlier borrowers). Forgiveness at 20 / 25 years respectively.
  • Can be $0/month at low income — but unpaid interest can accrue, unlike RAP.
  • Payment is capped at the 10-year Standard amount from when you entered.

Standard and Tiered Standard

  • Standard 10-year: fixed payment, highest monthly, lowest total interest, no forgiveness. For many borrowers this is the do-nothing default destination — check your servicer notice.
  • Tiered Standard (loans disbursed on/after July 1, 2026): fixed payment over 10–25 years depending on balance (<$25k → 10 yr, $25–50k → 15 yr, $50–100k → 20 yr, ≥$100k → 25 yr).

Family size vs. dependents — they are not the same number

Family size (drives IBR's protected income) counts you, your spouse, and everyone who receives more than half their support from you — it includes your dependents and yourself. Dependents (drives RAP's $50 credit) means tax dependents — the people you claim on your return. A married couple with two kids: family size 4, dependents 2. Undercounting family size overstates an IBR payment by roughly $71/month per missed person.

Pursuing PSLF? Rank plans by a different number.

For Public Service Loan Forgiveness, what matters is the total you pay through 120 qualifying payments — not a plan's run-to-term "total cost." A plan that looks cheapest over 25 years can cost a PSLF borrower thousands more by month 120. Two more things: verify your new plan keeps payments PSLF-qualifying before switching, and save your payment history before the transition — plan and servicer changes are when counts get lost. PSLF forgiveness is tax-free; ordinary IDR forgiveness is taxed as income.

Quick answers

When is my deadline?

It's personal: waves roll through December 2026, and your servicer's notice starts your ~90-day window. The notice is authoritative — not blogs, and not this page.

What happens if I do nothing?

You're assigned a plan by default under the July 2026 rules — for many borrowers that's a Standard-family plan with a much higher payment than an income-driven option. Verify the default named in your notice.

Can I switch back later?

Leaving SAVE or IBR for RAP is one-way under current rules. Treat the decision as permanent.

Is the forgiven amount taxed?

PSLF forgiveness: no. Ordinary IDR forgiveness (RAP's year-30, IBR's year-20/25): taxed as income under current law — plan for that bill.
