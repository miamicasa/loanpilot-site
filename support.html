Common questions

  • Why does my number differ from my servicer's? Projections assume flat income and on-time payments; your servicer uses your certified income and payment history. See how we compute for the exact formulas and our assumptions.
  • Where is my data stored? On your iPhone only. Use Export in the app to make a backup before switching phones — see the privacy policy.
  • How do I restore a purchase? Settings tab → Restore Purchases, signed into the same Apple ID.
  • Rules changed and the app shows a banner? That means an app update with new repayment rules is available — update from the App Store and the banner clears.

Found a wrong number?

We treat calculation reports as bugs of the highest severity. Email the inputs you used (no personal identifiers needed) and the number you expected — every formula is tested against the statute text, and we publish how each plan is computed.
